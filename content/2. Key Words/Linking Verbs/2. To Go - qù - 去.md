**Character:** 去

**Pinyin:** qù

**Meaning:** To go; leave; depart. To remove, or get rid of. Past / Gone

**Pronunciation Tip:** Choo or tyoo like pitting with a sharp falling tone. Or Chew 

**Uses:** (Wǒ **qù** xuéxiào.) - 我**去**学校。 (I **go to** school.)
	   (Bǎ zāng dōngxī **qù** diào.) 把脏东西**去**掉 - **Remove** the dirt.
	   (Zhè yǐjīng shì **qù**nián de shì le.) - 这已经是**去**年的事了 - This is already something from **last** year.
