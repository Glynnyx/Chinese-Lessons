**Character:** 会说中文吗？  
**Pinyin:** nǐ huì shuō zhōngwén ma  
**Meaning:** - Can you speak Chinese?  

**Character:** 会一点，我的中文不太好，不过，我正在学。  
**Pinyin:** huì yì diǎr, wǒ de zhōngwén bù tài hǎo, búguò, wǒ zhèng zài xué  
**Meaning:** - A little bit; my Chinese is not very good, but I’m learning.  

**Character:** 你学中文多久了？

**Meaning:** - How long have you been learning Chinese?

**Pinyin:** wǒ gāng kāishǐ xué, xué le yī liǎng gè yuè le
