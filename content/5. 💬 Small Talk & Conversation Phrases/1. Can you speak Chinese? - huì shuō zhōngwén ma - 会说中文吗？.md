**Character:** 会说中文吗？  
**Pinyin:** huì shuō zhōngwén ma  
**Meaning:** - Can you speak Chinese?  
**Pronunciation Tip:** “hway shwoh jong-wen ma?”

**Character:** 会一点，我的中文不太好，不过，我正在学。  
**Pinyin:** huì yì diǎn, wǒ de zhōngwén bù tài hǎo, búguò, wǒ zhèng zài xué  
**Meaning:** - A little bit; my Chinese is not very good, but I’m learning.  
**Pronunciation Tip:** Smooth and gentle pacing; tone shifts around "bù tài hǎo"
